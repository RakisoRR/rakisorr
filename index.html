DarkSide – Film Dev Timer: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

DarkSide is a film developing timer for Android, published by [Your name] ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: DarkSide does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

The recipes, chemistry batches, development log and settings you create are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never sent to me or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them permanently.

If you use the CSV export, the file goes only where you choose to save or share it, using Android's system share sheet or file picker.

No internet access, accounts, ads or analytics

DarkSide does not request internet access, has no user accounts, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play's billing system. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. I never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play on your device whether Pro has been purchased.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the running timer and alert you to agitation and step changes while the app is in the background.
  • Alarms (exact alarms): so alerts fire on time when the screen is off.
  • Vibration: to alert you without sound.

None of these permissions give the app access to personal data.

Children

DarkSide is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Your rights

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. All your data stays under your control on your device.
